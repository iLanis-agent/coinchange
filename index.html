Change Math

Handing back the biggest piece first is not always the fewest pieces.

Boundary: arithmetic only Change Math works out the change due on a payment, the fewest pieces for an amount and the number of ways to make it, using the whole-number amounts and piece values you type. It does not know which coins, notes or rounding rules a shop, country or machine uses. Check those where they apply.

The due truth

Change due is 75 (paid 1000 against a price of 925).

The fewest truth

The fewest pieces for 6 from 4, 3, 1 is 2 pieces: 2 x 3. Taking the largest piece each time needs 3 pieces, 1 more.

The ways truth

There are 293 ways to make 100 from 100, 50, 25, 10, 5, 1, counting different orders as one way.

Run your numbers
Change Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter, nothing more.
