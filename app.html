Change Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out the change due on a payment, the fewest pieces for an amount and the number of ways to make it, using the whole-number amounts and piece values you type. It does not know which coins, notes or rounding rules a shop, country or machine uses. Check those where they apply.

The due truth

What comes back from a payment. Amounts are whole numbers of the smallest unit, such as cents.

The fewest truth

The fewest pieces for an amount, against always taking the largest piece. Type your own piece values, separated by spaces or commas.

The ways truth

How many different mixes of pieces make an amount. Order does not count.

Change Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
